Turning Imagination Into Visual Luxury

I am a Communication student at St. Joseph College of Communication specializing in Graphic Design, Digital Illustration, and Visual Media. From authoring and illustrating a 12-page children's storybook (The Sea Book) and designing the "ചായാ (Chaya)" specialty tea shop menu to crafting brand identities like Magic City and tetra-pack designs for BABA Land, I love blending storytelling with modern visual aesthetics.

Beyond visual graphics, I write original Malayalam lyrics and produce hip-hop and background scores in FL Studio, bringing a complete audio-visual perspective to every project.
